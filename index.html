ABOUT AIRBORNE CHALLENGE

AIrborne Challenge is an AI-powered 3D flight game featuring a real-time voice-activated AI Flight Assistant powered by Gemini Live API.

🎮
Dynamic Rules
Every flight has unique, secret scoring multipliers for shapes, colors, sizes, and altitudes.
🎙️
Voice Flight AI
Speak directly to Gemini Live using push-to-talk (hold T) to get flight guidance and strategic hints.
⚡
Real-Time Tool Calling
Your AI flight assistant can highlight targets in 3D space, alter aircraft physics, change weather, and execute tricks!
⏱️
2-Minute Dash
Maximize your score before the clock runs out and claim your spot on the leaderboard.

Master your aircraft controls and talk with your AI flight assistant:

  • W / S : Aircraft Pitch (Up / Down)
  • A / D : Aircraft Turn (Left / Right)
  • SHIFT : Speed Boost
  • SPACE : Air Brake
  • T : Push-To-Talk Radio (Hold to speak to Gemini)
  • ESC : Exit Flight & Return to Main Menu

Built with modern web standards and Google GenAI infrastructure:

Firebase AI SDK
Gemini Live API
Three.js 3D
Vite SPA
Web Audio API
